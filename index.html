Course for the team

AI App Building Framework

Three chapters. A working picture of AI, the tech stack this teaching app is built with, then the research you do before building the app for users. The app is how the team learns.

How to use this course

The team does not need to train a model. Everyone needs shared language for the decisions ahead, and a clear map of which tool owns which job. Product managers use chapter 3 to frame the buyer, the price, and the market.

The chapters

  1. Chapter 1 · AI 101 Rules, neural networks, Transformers, LLMs, RAG, and agentic AI — in the order each idea became necessary.
  2. Chapter 2 · The tech stack GitHub Actions, Firebase, Firestore, Neon, Stripe, and Capacitor. One web app, also packaged for Android and iOS.
  3. Chapter 3 · Research before you build Research before building for users. Pricing and viability (TAM, SAM, SOM) are ready. Steps 1, 2, and 3 of this chapter are next.
