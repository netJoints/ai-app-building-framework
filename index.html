Course for the team

AI App Building Framework

Four chapters. A working picture of AI, the tech stack this teaching app is built with, the research before building for users, then how product managers write a prompt. The app is how the team learns.

How to use this course

The team does not need to train a model. Everyone needs shared language for the decisions ahead, and a clear map of which tool owns which job. Product managers use chapter 3 for the buyer, the price, and the market, and chapter 4 to write the prompt.

The chapters

  1. Chapter 1 · AI 101 Rules, neural networks, Transformers, LLMs, RAG, and agentic AI — in the order each idea became necessary.
  2. Chapter 2 · The tech stack GitHub Actions, Firebase, Firestore, Neon, Stripe, and Capacitor. One web app, also packaged for Android and iOS.
  3. Chapter 3 · Research before you build Research before building for users. Pricing and viability are ready, including TAM, SAM, SOM, and break-even cost. Steps 1, 2, and 3 of this chapter are next.
  4. Chapter 4 · Prompt engineering How product managers write one prompt to build a product or research a feature idea. Objective, Role Map, four agents, evidence standard, stop rule, and output.
