Pricing

A price is a research result. It is the amount a specific buyer will pay for a specific outcome, compared with what they do today. Pick the number after the conversations, and write the assumption next to it.

Answer four questions before anyone builds the payment flow.

  • Who pays. The person using the app, a manager buying for a group, or someone else.
  • What they pay for. A seat, a workspace, a result, or usage.
  • When they pay. Once, on a subscription, or by use.
  • What they do instead. The current workaround, and what it costs in money and time. That comparison is what the buyer is actually making.

On this stack, Stripe holds charges, subscriptions, and payment state. Firebase Functions creates the charge. Card details and provider keys stay with Stripe and in GitHub Actions secrets. They do not go in the repository or in the mobile app.

For product managers. Talk to people in the niche before the build. Ask what they pay now, what they tried, and what would make them switch. Record the answers. A price that has never been said out loud to a buyer is still a guess.
5

Viability

Viability asks whether the market you can actually win is large enough to justify building. Three nested markets keep the claim honest. Each one is smaller than the one before it.

Term Name What it counts
TAM Total Addressable Market Everyone who could buy this kind of product, with no niche filter.
SAM Serviceable Addressable Market The part of the TAM that matches this product’s specific niche: the buyers this product can actually serve.
SOM Serviceable Obtainable Market The part of the SAM the team can realistically reach and win. Serviceable, and obtainable.
TAM · total market → SAM · our niche → SOM · what we can win

Write four things beside every number: who is counted, the time window, the source, and the filter that removed everyone else. A TAM with no source is a slogan. A SOM with no time window is a wish.

For product managers. The build decision uses SOM, not TAM. Multiply that obtainable market by the price from step 4, and compare it with the cost to build and run the app. A huge total market can still hide a niche the team cannot reach.
